# Reference sites

When the user shares sites or screenshots they like ("make it feel like these"), study them properly, then borrow their **qualities**, not their looks. A copy of someone else's site is its own kind of slop.

PLUGIN below means the plugin root, two folders above this skill's base directory.

## Study them

1. Run `node "PLUGIN/scripts/references.mjs" <url or image> [more ...]`. For each site it saves screenshots (top, middle, lower), dismisses cookie banners, and records how it's built: heading and body fonts, headline size, GSAP, smooth scroll, WebGL/3D, video, custom cursor, running animations, platform.
2. Open the one contact sheet it makes (`.acquired-taste/references/sheet.png`). Don't open every screenshot.
3. Write down in `.acquired-taste/design-system.md`, under **References**, for each site: the 2-3 specific qualities worth borrowing for *this* brief, and what *not* to take. Be concrete:
   - "Axiom: one restrained headline over full-bleed moody product photography; mono micro-labels for status; huge stat numerals"
   - "FPS: an italic serif word inside a heavy sans headline; playful collage hero"
   - not "clean and modern"

## Use them

- Let the borrowed qualities shape the design read: concept spine, signature moment, type twist, motion character.
- Translate, don't transplant. Use this business's content, brand, colours and voice; keep only the craft idea. Never reuse their copy, images, logos or exact layouts.
- Only borrow what fits the site type. A dashboard can take a reference's typography and restraint, but not its custom cursor or scroll effects.
- At the final review, compare your screenshots with the reference sheet: does the site feel as finished, as confident and as alive? Name the biggest remaining gap and close it if you can.

## No references given

Use the finish bar in `review.md`. It was distilled from well-crafted sites: a signature visual made for the brand, type with one twist, purposeful motion, depth, and small alive details.
