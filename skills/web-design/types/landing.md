# Landing pages and marketing sites

**Success:** within seconds a visitor understands what this is, trusts it, knows what to do next, and remembers one thing about it.

**Starting dials:** layout variance 7, motion 6, density 4 (out of 10). Adjust to the brand: a bank sits calmer, a design studio bolder.

## The template to avoid

The standard SaaS sequence: a hero with a phone mockup, a logo bar, 3 or 4 identical feature cards, "1-2-3 steps", a testimonial, two-tier pricing with a "most popular" badge, an FAQ accordion and a CTA band. Also: a small uppercase label above every section heading, and big empty image boxes. Plain AI output lands here almost every time. It works, but it never looks finished or memorable.

## Build it instead from

- **The story this business needs to tell.** Start from the audience's problem and the one claim that wins them. Choose sections that serve that story; drop any that don't.
- **A hero that fits the viewport** at 1440×900 and at 390×844: a headline with personality (ideally under ~8 words), a short supporting line, one primary action (plus an optional secondary one), and the signature visual.
- **One section that demonstrates the product for real:** an interactive or animated fragment of the actual feature, not a feature list.
- **Varied section composition:** full-bleed, split, editorial column, sticky scroll story, horizontal sequence, oversized type. Don't repeat a layout more than twice in a row, and don't put three side-by-side cards everywhere.
- **Proof that's real** (see `craft/honesty.md`): the user's numbers, quotes and facts only.
- **Pricing and FAQ** only when the brief has real prices and real questions. Present them in the site's own style, not as default components.
- **A considered ending:** the last section and footer feel designed, not leftover.

## Motion for this type

- 2-3 signature moments: the hero entrance, one scroll-driven moment, tactile hover and press states.
- Choreograph them: a deliberate order and shared timing, spring or custom easing, never linear.
- Fade-up on everything isn't motion design.

## Checks

- The hero fits without scrolling at 1440×900 and 390×844.
- The primary action is visible above the fold on mobile.
- No section layout repeats more than twice in a row.
